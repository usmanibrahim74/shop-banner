Shop banner · 12 ft × 2 ft · Shaizy × Boson Studio

Fit

Urdu lines stay at least 1.6 in above the bottom edge, clear of eyelets and the hem. Print guides show a 1 in edge margin (dashed) and eyelets every 2 ft along the top and bottom edges. These are only a preview aid and are not part of the SVG files. All colours sit inside the CMYK range of flex printers, and all text is outlined, so no fonts are needed at the print shop. To change the text, edit source/build_banner.py and run it again.
